    ## Findings routed to Session 2

    1. **HIGH / contract correction: primary hit target shrinks during idle animation.** The actual accessible SPIN rectangles measure 55.56–55.87 logical pixels high even though the layout minimum is 56. `StageBreath` scales the entire interactive subtree. Keep the interaction and semantic bounds at least 56 pixels while moving decorative animation inside. Confirm through an animation-cycle geometry regression and Chrome remeasurement. This is a small local fix, but the explicit minimum must hold.
    2. **MEDIUM / composition: desktop host crop floats.** At 1440×900 the source torso ends in a sharp horizontal edge above the lower wing. Ground that edge at the viewport boundary or an intentional foreground boundary without reducing the play field. The lobby has the same source cutoff; review its grounding while maintaining readable, accessible actions.
    3. **Verification limitation: one external fallback font request.** Despite locally served CanvasKit and bundled Rye/Nunito Sans, Chrome requested a Roboto WOFF2 from `fonts.gstatic.com` with HTTP 200. Do not claim network-independent startup from this run. Inspect the Flutter/bootstrap or theme fallback and test a fresh cache with external traffic blocked after addressing it.
