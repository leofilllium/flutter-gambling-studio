# Learning proposal: Require themed multiplier balls in every store screenshot set

Status: proposed; human review and merge required.

## Observed problem

Store-screenshots guidance could gate or omit the requested five themed multiplier balls when a game's paytable lacked one of the values.

## Proposed improvement

Make the five store-only multiplier balls unconditional in store screenshot art while preserving real gameplay captures and math.
