# Learning proposal: Use restrained theme-led store grading by default

Status: proposed; human review and merge required.

## Observed problem

The store-screenshot workflow hard-codes bright, highly saturated output and the user rejected the resulting carousel as too bright and saturated.

## Proposed improvement

Make moderate theme-led color the default, remove mandatory brightness/glare and numeric saturation floors, and reserve vivid or bright grading for explicit Design DNA or user direction.

Base commit: `da798a33fa2b5bf8a3a1c3e0dd4d46991930b402`

    Observation: A user rejected the latest Zeus store carousel as too bright and too
    saturated and asked that a lightly saturated, non-bright treatment become the usual
    store-screenshot default.

    Reproduction: The current store-screenshots runbook requires mean saturation >=0.68,
    targets 0.78-0.88, calls for a broad bright far plane and controlled glare, and its
    example final exports use `--pop max`. The compositor help says `max` finishes toward
    0.80 mean saturation and uses bloom to read brighter at thumbnail size.

    Measurement: Re-exporting the same recovered Zeus key-art source with no geometry
    change at 440x956 per panel produced these whole-carousel averages:

    - `--pop off`: mean HSV saturation 0.442, mean luminance 0.666, 15.2% pixels above
      0.90 luminance.
    - `--pop max`: mean HSV saturation 0.598, mean luminance 0.729, 22.0% pixels above
      0.90 luminance.

    The mandatory max grade therefore raised mean saturation by 35%, mean luminance by
    9%, and the near-white highlight share by 45% for this real output. This compounded
    an already luminous source and produced the rejected look.

    Requested improvement: Make the normal store-art treatment moderately saturated and
    theme-led, without a mandatory brightness, glare, or numeric saturation floor. Keep
    strong contrast, readable silhouettes, and controlled highlights. Reserve vivid,
    bright, or high-bloom grading for an explicit game Design DNA or user request, and
    audit the assembled carousel at thumbnail and full size before delivery.

    Scope: Store artwork grading defaults and review gates. This does not desaturate
    runtime assets, weaken topology/seam/readability checks, or prohibit bright/vivid art
    when it is part of the game-specific brief.
