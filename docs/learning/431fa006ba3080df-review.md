# Store multiplier placement guidance review

Observed failure: a Joker store panorama placed five themed multiplier balls together in the
lower prop band. The user's revised composition keeps the balls flying separately around the
second and third panels and uses real game objects for the irregular lower spill. The accepted
App Store and Play eight-slide sets passed their independent store shape checks and strict
panorama art gates. In the Play crop, the upper rims of x5 and x50 are slightly clipped, but
their inscriptions and physical ball forms remain readable.
