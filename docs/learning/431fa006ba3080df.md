# Learning proposal: Separate flying multiplier balls from the lower object spill

Status: proposed; human review and merge required.

## Observed problem

Store panorama guidance allowed themed multiplier balls to cluster in the bottom object heap, obscuring the intended flying reward composition.

## Proposed improvement

Require context-aware airborne spacing and distinct lower real-object spill in planning, generation, and final App Store/Play crop review.

Base commit: `4b2e25938c0b221da638a0d699b73de83ab78730`

    Observation (2026-09-23): A generated three-panel Joker slots store panorama placed the themed x5/x10/x25/x50/x100 balls together at the lower foreground, where they read as part of the asset heap.     Existing guidance required all five balls and a varied object spill but did not distinguish the balls from that spill or require airborne spacing. Object/mechanic-led layouts still need context-sensitive placement around their actual play surface.

    Remedy: update the existing store-screenshots skill's planning, composition and final-crop review. Require balls to read as separate flying scene objects, not as a bottom row or heap; spread them around game action with clear space, primarily panels 2-3 for a character-led triptych. Keep real sprite assets as the irregular lower spill. Verify label visibility and spacing after both target crops and gutters, not solely in the raw generated image. Preserve topology, character, game math and authentic capture rules.
