# Learning proposal: Require lower-spill visibility checks in object-led store carousels

Status: proposed; human review and merge required.

## Observed problem

The third panel of an object-led store panorama could look sparse after mobile cropping even though the source contained foreground objects.

## Proposed improvement

Clarify the store-screenshot runbook so the final App Store and Google Play crops explicitly confirm that identifiable lower game objects remain in frame for object/mechanic-led panoramas.

    # Store carousel crop-density learning evidence

    ## Observation

    During a review of the Shining Crown store carousel, the third panorama panel
    appeared sparse and its lower game objects were not sufficiently visible after
    mobile crop. The requested revision also identified empty airborne regions.

    ## Verification

    The first Google Play crop diagnostic reported a third-panel lower-band ratio
    of 0.88 against the upper band, so it was not released. After a targeted
    regeneration that added a visible lower object spill and restrained airborne
    support, the final Google Play diagnostic reported 1.01 and the final
    App Store and Google Play carousel previews were reviewed individually. The
    published crops visibly retain the crown, key/scepter, bell, heart, gems and
    reward coins in the lower frame; panel three also retains the supported x5
    reward coin.

    ## Bounded inference

    The store-screenshot runbook already requires readable content in every crop,
    but it does not make the object/mechanic-specific lower-spill check explicit
    enough for an owner-facing panorama review. A short clarification should make
    the exact output-size check and visible lower-object confirmation unambiguous
    without replacing its existing composition or art gates.
